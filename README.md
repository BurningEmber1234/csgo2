# BREACH — Tactical Operations

A browser-based first-person tactical shooter built with Three.js.

## Armory

Press **B** to open the field-buy menu. Purchases equip immediately; the training round starts with **$16,000** and pays cash for eliminations and round results. Search the full catalog or filter by category. You can also open the armory from **Loadout** before deploying.

The catalog includes all CS2 buyable firearms and the Zeus x27: Glock-18, P2000, USP-S, Dual Berettas, P250, Five-SeveN, Tec-9, CZ75-Auto, Desert Eagle, R8 Revolver, MAC-10, MP9, MP7, MP5-SD, UMP-45, P90, PP-Bizon, Galil AR, FAMAS, AK-47, M4A4, M4A1-S, AUG, SG 553, SSG 08, AWP, G3SG1, SCAR-20, Nova, XM1014, MAG-7, Sawed-Off, M249, Negev, and Zeus x27. The requested **Marshal** is included as a custom scoped rifle.

Hold **right mouse button** to scope with the Marshal, AWP, SSG 08, G3SG1, SCAR-20, AUG, or SG 553.

## Maps and rounds

Vote from the lobby's **MAP VOTE** tab (or the active map card) for Dust Sector, Frostline, or Harbor Front. You can change your vote until you lock it; changing to a different map starts a fresh match. Each new round begins with a five-second freeze-time countdown, then displays **FIGHT**. Five CTs line up at CT spawn and five hostiles line up at T spawn, side-by-side.

## Controls

- **W A S D** — Move
- **Mouse** — Aim
- **Left click** — Fire
- **R** — Reload
- **Right mouse button** — Scope (optic weapons)
- **Shift** — Walk
- **Space** — Jump
- **B** — Open/close buy menu
- **Esc** — Pause / close the buy menu
